# Table, properties and layers

- Click a feature on the map. Its properties show in the side panel, and the feature is outlined.
- Table opens the attribute table under the map. Type in the filter box to keep only matching rows. Click a row to zoom to that feature. Zoom to rows fits the map to the filtered rows.
- Layers lists KML folders, TopoJSON objects, GPX tracks and waypoints, or geometry types. Untick a layer to hide it.
- Copy GeoJSON copies the selected feature as GeoJSON in longitude/latitude.
- Click the coordinates in the status line to copy the latitude and longitude under the cursor.
